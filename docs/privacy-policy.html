Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

OutStep ("the app") is a step-count competition app for iOS. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Step count data, read from Apple Health (HealthKit) on your device. We only read your step count — the app never writes anything to Health.
  • Your display name, which you choose when you first sign in.
  • Your Apple ID account identifier, via Sign in with Apple, used only to identify your account.

How your Health data is used

Your daily step counts are shown to other participants in the challenges you choose to join, so you can compare progress and compete with friends. That's the only use.

  • We do not sell your Health data to anyone, including advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your Health data for advertising, marketing, or any purpose other than showing it on leaderboards within challenges you've joined.
  • We do not share your Health data with any third party outside the app.

Who can see your data

Your display name and step counts are visible only to other people who are participants of the same challenge as you. They are never visible to anyone outside a challenge you've joined, and are never made public.

Where your data is stored

App data (your display name, account identifier, and daily step counts) is stored using Supabase, a backend hosting provider, with access restrictions in place so users can only read data for challenges they participate in.

Deleting your data

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email us at the address below.

Children's privacy

OutStep is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
